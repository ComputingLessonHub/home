About the block editor

The block coding tasks in these lessons use the Scratch 3 editor, made by the Scratch Foundation. It runs entirely in your browser. You do not need a Scratch account.

What is saved

Your scripts, sprites and variables are saved with your lesson work, along with any costumes you draw yourself. Sprites, backdrops and sounds from the Scratch library are not copied: the editor fetches them again from Scratch's library when you open your project.

In a lesson you cannot upload your own pictures or sounds or record sounds, so that every project stays small enough to save. Press Download on the task to take a copy of your project home as a .sb3 file, which opens in Scratch.
