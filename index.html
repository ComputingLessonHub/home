How to sign in

Type in your computer username and press Continue, then type your password underneath.

If this is your first time, you will be asked to pick a password of your own straight after pressing Continue.

If you have forgotten your password, press Forgot password and put your hand up, so your teacher can let you back in.

Something not working?

This clears anything left over in this browser and loads the page again. Your saved work is not deleted.

Your username

Your username is the same as your computer username. It is made of three parts:

Smithyour surname Jfirst letter of your first name 26the year you started here

So John Smith, who started at the school in 2026, is SmithJ26.
